## D2m: close a station
**20 registered closures:** the 10 nodes most stepped on by the 600 chases, ties by handle, then 10 by seed (7 on some route, 3 on none). **600 chases:** the file's up to 400, then seeded to 600. One closure a unit on a fresh machine: `close`, all 600, `reopen`, all 600. **Bar, as written: 100% of affected answers change to the re-solved answer, 100% of unaffected answers are byte-identical, and reopening restores all 600 for every closure.** Same per-call checks as D4m.
**Both machine exams:** executor free-running, with the reference machine beside it on every call, Mac CPU, 6 workers, each under memguard. Rehearsal, c024: 0.41 s a chase, 1.0 s a `path`, about 0.12 ms a chip token, on 3 workers. **Order:** `paths` first, scored; then `base,close`, scored; a miss stops the next (`exam/go.sh`). **Budget, from the predicted tokens:** D4m 500 × 3 s × 1.5 / 6 = 375 s, `--limit-s 700`. D2m 24,640 × 1.05 s × 1.5 / 6 = 6,468 s, `--limit-s 7,000`, which gates a unit's start. The items build stops on any gold or oracle disagreement; a file chase over the capture window is dropped, counted and reported. Results to `exam/results/nyc/`.
